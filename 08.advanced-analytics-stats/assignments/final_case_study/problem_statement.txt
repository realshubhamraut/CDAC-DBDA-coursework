Consumer Lending Risk Insights Through Data-Driven Analytics

1. Background & Business Context
Consumer finance companies face significant challenges when lending money to individuals with limited, insufficient, or non-existent credit history. A lack of credit information increases the uncertainty involved in predicting whether a customer will repay the loan on time.
Loan defaults lead to direct financial losses and increase the company’s risk exposure. Conversely, incorrectly rejecting a potentially good customer results in lost business opportunities. Therefore, data-driven decision-making is essential for:
Reducing credit risk


Improving loan approval strategies


Identifying high-risk applicants early


Setting appropriate credit limits and interest rates


Your role is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Statistical Hypothesis Testing to uncover the drivers of loan default using real-world customer data.
The assignment simulates work typically done by analysts in risk management teams at banks, NBFCs, and fintech lenders.

2. Objective of the Case Study
The main aim is to:
Understand the customer attributes and loan characteristics
Identify behavioral and demographic variables that drive default
Discover patterns that differentiate “defaulters” from “non-defaulters”
Use hypothesis testing to validate whether observed patterns are statistically significant
Provide actionable recommendations that reduce credit risk
You must work with two provided datasets that capture customer information at the time of applying for the loan and their previous loan histories.

3. Dataset Description
You are provided with three files:

3.1 Dataset 1 — credit_risk_applicants.csv

a. Target variable

NAME_EDUCATION_TYPE

Variables such as:
APARTMENTS_AVG, YEARS_BUILD_AVG, LIVINGAREA_AVG, etc.
 provide information on the living conditions and real estate characteristics.
